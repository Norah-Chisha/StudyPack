        About and limits

        StudyPack turns your own notes into a study pack you can use offline. You connect once to create a pack, then study with no internet.

        Please know

        • Only text-based PDFs can be read. Scanned or image-only pages are skipped.
        • AI can make mistakes. Always check explanations against your lecturer's material.
        • When you create a pack, your notes are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini). Do not include personal information. On the free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products.
        • Packs are stored only on this device. Share a pack only if you have the right to share the material it came from.
        • "Source verified" means the quoted passage was found word for word in your notes. It does not prove the whole explanation is correct.
        • This is a prototype. It has not yet been tested with a large group of students.
